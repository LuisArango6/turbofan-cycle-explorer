Particle colours are calculated values; their speed is illustrative, and the core is drawn denser than its real share of the flow (1 part in BPR + 1). Rotors turn 750 times slower than the Trent 1000 shaft speeds (LP 2,683, IP 8,937, HP 13,391 rpm), keeping their ratio. Stylised geometry, not manufacturer data.
